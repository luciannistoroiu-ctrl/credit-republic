frequently asked questions

what you need to know.

is the commission really 0 fees for me?

yes. the brokerage service is free for you. according to the current legislation, we are remunerated by the financial institution for the volume of mediated credits. no analysis fee, no honorarium.

does the prequalification affect my credit score?

no. the algorithm works independently. the first official inquiry to the credit bureau takes place only at the end, with your agreement, when you submit the file to the bank.

what is a mortgage broker?

an authorized credit intermediary. they work with all banks, know the grid of each one and take the file where your profile fits. the approval decision remains with the bank. they are paid by the bank, not by you.

am i obligated to move forward?

no. the prequalification is free and made to inform you. the decision remains yours, at every step.

what data do i need to enter?

general information about income and current obligations. no documents uploaded, no access to the bank account. the data is secured.
